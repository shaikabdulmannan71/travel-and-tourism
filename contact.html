Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our luxury booking and concierge procedures.

What makes King's Travels different from standard travel agencies? +
We exclusively specialize in ultra-luxury, high-comfort journeys. Every client is assigned a dedicated senior curator, 24/7 personal on-ground assistance, private luxury vehicles, and hand-selected five-star palace suites with VIP inclusions.
Can you customize existing tour packages? +
Absolutely. Every package featured on our website serves as a curated baseline. You can adjust duration, upgrade to private villas or presidential suites, charter private helicopters, or extend with multi-country stops.
How does the payment and deposit process work? +
Upon finalizing your customized itinerary, a 30% deposit secures all five-star accommodations, private aviation, and luxury permits. The remaining balance is payable 21 days prior to departure.
Do you arrange private jet charters and helicopter transfers? +
Yes. We have licensed global aviation partners for private Gulfstream, Bombardier, and helicopter transfers across the Swiss Alps, Dubai, Kashmir, and island archipelagos.
